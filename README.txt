Fade Chess – the website
========================
This folder IS the website. Upload it as it is to any static web host
(index.html must stay at the top level, next to the "data" folder and cover.jpg).

Before going live:
  1. imprint.html  – replace the bracketed placeholders with your details.
  2. privacy.html  – name your hosting provider and your contact address.
  3. Open the site once on a phone and once on a computer and solve a puzzle.

Files
  index.html                the trainer
  cover.jpg                 the banner picture
  data/band-*.json          10,500 puzzles in 21 rating bands (600 to 2600+), loaded as a player needs them
  credits.html              sources and licences (keep it linked: the piece set requires attribution)
  privacy.html, imprint.html
  pieces/                   the two extra piece styles (Modern, Fantasy)
  LICENSE-*.txt, COPYRIGHT-*.txt   licence texts of the three piece sets (keep them in the folder)
