Puzzles

All puzzles come from the Lichess puzzle database, which Lichess releases under the Creative Commons CC0 licence. Each puzzle links to its page on lichess.org. This site is not affiliated with Lichess. The selection covers ratings from about 600 to 2600 and above.

The cover artwork and the default "Fade" piece set were generated with an AI image tool.

Chess pieces

You can choose between five piece styles in Settings.

  • Fade: the house style of Fade Chess, made for this site. The artwork was generated with an AI image tool (Google Gemini) from our own brief and then cut out and prepared for the board.
  • Classic (default): the chess pieces by Colin M.L. Burnett as published on Wikimedia Commons, where the author offers them under a choice of GFDL, BSD or GPL; used here under the BSD licence. The files are the web-optimised copies distributed by Lichess. Licence text
  • Standard: "Papercut" by Nikolay Anzarov, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). The files are the web-optimised copies distributed by Lichess; the drawings are unchanged.
  • Rounded: "Kosal" by philatype, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). Changes: the style rules of each file were moved into the shapes so the pieces can be embedded in the page; the drawings are unchanged. Licence text.
  • Modern: "Chessnut" by Alexis Luengas, licensed under the Apache License 2.0. The files are the web-optimised copies distributed by Lichess; the drawings are unchanged. Licence text · copyright notice.

Learning methods

  • The adaptive mode orders puzzles by accuracy and response time, an approach described in research by Everett Mettler and Philip Kellman on adaptive response-time-based sequencing.
  • The spaced-repetition mode schedules reviews with the FSRS algorithm (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler) by the open-spaced-repetition project.
