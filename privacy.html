What is stored, and where

Your settings, your progress and the log of solved puzzles are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are not sent to us. You can delete them at any time under Settings → "Delete progress" or by clearing the browser's data for this site.

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking.
